\begin{abstract}
LatentMAS runs three non-decoding agents that grow a shared KV cache
(``relay''), then a Judger that decodes text from it. Empirically that relay is
\textbf{content-insensitive within task} (wrong-question $\approx$
right-question) and therefore highly redundant. On GSM8K we reduce relay
storage from $\sim\!100\,\mathrm{MB}/640$ positions to
$\sim\!2.5\,\mathrm{MB}/16$ positions ($\approx 40\times$) at matched
accuracy. Compression itself costs only $\sim\!20\,\mathrm{ms}$ per item
(tensor ops over the cache)---negligible versus upstream latent forwards
($\sim\!3\,\mathrm{s}$) and more than repaid by faster Judger attention over a
short prefix. Harder tasks (MATH, MedQA) remain compressible only at thicker
residual budgets.
\end{abstract}

\section{System model}

\subsection{Pipeline}

LatentMAS factorizes answering into four roles sharing one model:
\[
x
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Planner}\;}
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Critic}\;}
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Refiner}\;}
  C_{\mathrm{relay}}
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Judger}\;}
  y_{1:T}.
\]
Upstream agents do \textbf{not} emit tokens. Agent $a$ at latent step $t$
computes
\begin{align*}
h_t^{(a)}
  &= f_\theta\!\bigl(W_a(h_{t-1}^{(a)}),\; C_{<t}\bigr), \\
C_t
  &= C_{t-1}\,\oplus\,\mathrm{KV}\!\bigl(h_t^{(a)}\bigr),
\end{align*}
where $W_a$ realigns last-layer hidden states into the embedding space and
$\oplus$ appends new key/value slots. After $K$ steps per upstream agent (plus
role prefills), the Judger receives $C_{\mathrm{relay}}$ and runs ordinary
autoregressive decoding.

Default regime here: $K{=}40$ latent steps, greedy Judger decode, generation
budgets $T_{\max}$ task-dependent (GSM8K $768$, MATH $2048$, \ldots).

\subsection{What ``relay size'' means}

For a cache with $L$ layers, $n_{\mathrm{kv}}$ KV heads, head dim $d_h$, and
$S$ positions, byte cost is
\[
\mathrm{Bytes}(C)
  = 2\,L\,n_{\mathrm{kv}}\,S\,d_h\cdot b,
\]
with $b{=}2$ bytes for bf16/fp16. On Qwen3-14B
($L{=}40$, $n_{\mathrm{kv}}{=}8$, $d_h{=}128$):
\[
\mathrm{MB}(S)
  \approx \frac{2\cdot 40\cdot 8\cdot 128\cdot S\cdot 2}{2^{20}}
  = \frac{S}{6.4}\,\mathrm{MB}
  \quad\Rightarrow\quad
  S{=}640 \mapsto \approx 100\,\mathrm{MB}.
\]
Measured GSM8K means: $S\approx 640$, $\approx 100.0\,\mathrm{MB}$.

\section{Prior negative result (why we compress)}

Activation steering of upstream agents (SEAL / KV / learned CES contrasts) did
\textbf{not} improve accuracy. Linear probes of upstream latents for final
correctness are near-chance (AUCs $\approx 0.54$--$0.62$), while Judger states
are informative ($\approx 0.69$--$0.80$). So the relay is a poor place to
``inject correctness.''

Mechanism controls instead show a \textbf{scaffold} picture:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
\toprule
Relay given to Judger & Typical finding \\
\midrule
Real $C(x)$ & Baseline acc / length \\
Shuffled $C(x')$, $x'\neq x$
  & Acc $\approx$ real (within a few pts on GSM8K/MATH) \\
Empty & Acc drops (esp.\ MedQA: $0.63\to 0.27$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Interpretation: within a task family, $C$ supplies a structured, mostly
\textbf{instance-agnostic} prior that shortens / stabilizes Judger decoding
more than it transmits a lossless solution trace. High within-task redundancy
$\Rightarrow$ aggressive position selection should be viable.

\section{Compressor}

\subsection{Formal description}

Let layer $\ell$ store
$K^{(\ell)}, V^{(\ell)}\in\mathbb{R}^{1\times n_{\mathrm{kv}}\times S\times d_h}$.
Choose budget $B$, sink $s$, optional rank $r$, importance $\sigma$.

\paragraph{Scores.}
Default \texttt{key\_norm}, aggregated over heads so one index set is
compatible with a dense HF \texttt{DynamicCache}:
\[
\sigma_i
  = \sum_{h=1}^{n_{\mathrm{kv}}}
      \bigl\|K^{(\ell)}_{h,i,:}\bigr\|_2,
\qquad i=0,\ldots,S-1.
\]
Alternatives: value-norm, or recency $\sigma_i=i$.

\paragraph{Selection (plain-H / \texttt{evict}).}
\[
\mathcal{I}
  = \{0,\ldots,s-1\}
    \;\cup\;
    \operatorname{Top}_{B-s}
    \bigl(\{\sigma_i: i\ge s\}\bigr).
\]
Sort $\mathcal{I}$ ascending (preserve causal order) and restrict
\[
K' = K_{:,:,\mathcal{I},:},\qquad V' = V_{:,:,\mathcal{I},:}.
\]

\paragraph{OBF backfill (\texttt{obf}).}
Let $\mathcal{E}=\{0,\ldots,S-1\}\setminus\mathcal{I}$.
Partition $\mathcal{E}$ (in original order) into $r$ contiguous buckets and
mean-pool each bucket to get synthetic slots
$\widetilde K,\widetilde V\in\mathbb{R}^{1\times n_{\mathrm{kv}}\times r\times d_h}$.
Append:
\[
K_{\mathrm{out}} = [K'\,\|\,\widetilde K],\qquad
V_{\mathrm{out}} = [V'\,\|\,\widetilde V].
\]
Output length: $B$ (\texttt{evict}) or $B{+}r$ (\texttt{obf}).
Default knobs in the headline GSM8K run: $B{=}16$, $s{=}4$, $r{=}8$
$\Rightarrow$ $16$ or $24$ positions.

\paragraph{Complexity.}
One pass of norms + \texttt{topk} + \texttt{index\_select} (+ cheap mean-pool):
\[
\Theta\!\bigl(L\,n_{\mathrm{kv}}\,S\,d_h\bigr)
  = \Theta\bigl(\mathrm{numel}(C)\bigr),
\]
i.e.\ linear in cache size---\textbf{not} another model forward.

\paragraph{Faithfulness caveat.}
Published H-OBF selects per-head via attention mass. We select \textbf{per
layer} (shared indices across heads) so the result remains a standard dense
cache consumable by \texttt{generate}. Scorer is pluggable.

\subsection{Placement in the pipeline}
\[
C_{\mathrm{relay}}
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Compress}_{B,s,r}\;}
  \widehat C
  \xrightarrow{\;\mathrm{Judger}\,\pm\,\mathrm{ASC}\;}
  y.
\]
Upstream agents are always unsteered. Optional \textbf{ASC} $=$ Judger-only
SEAL residual at layer $28$, coefficient $40$, for length control.

\section{Cost model --- including compression overhead}

End-to-end latency per item in the harness is
\[
t_{\mathrm{e2e}}
  = t_{\mathrm{upstream}}
  + t_{\mathrm{compress}}
  + t_{\mathrm{decode}}.
\]

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Term & What it is & Scales with \\
\midrule
$t_{\mathrm{upstream}}$
  & $3(K{+}1)$ latent forwards + prefills
  & model FLOPs $\times K$ \\
$t_{\mathrm{compress}}$
  & norms / top-$k$ / gather / optional pool
  & $\mathrm{numel}(C)$ \\
$t_{\mathrm{decode}}$
  & Judger AR decode; attends over prefix $|\widehat C|$
  & $\approx\sum_t O(L\,n_h\,t_{\mathrm{ctx}}\,d)$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{Measured overhead (GSM8K, keep-$16$, seed 123, $n{=}60$)}

From \texttt{report.json} means and paired contrasts:

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}llrrrrrr@{}}
\toprule
Arm & Relay & Acc & Tok & $t_{\mathrm{dec}}$ & $t_{\mathrm{e2e}}$ & MB & Peak \\
\midrule
A & full & 0.817 & 529 & 2.84\,s & 5.87\,s & 100.0 & 36.8\,GB \\
C & obf ($B{+}r{=}24$) & 0.800 & 567 & 1.79\,s & 4.85\,s & 3.75 & 34.2\,GB \\
E & evict ($B{=}16$) & 0.817 & 562 & 1.78\,s & 4.82\,s & \textbf{2.50} & 34.1\,GB \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Paired contrasts ($C{-}A$):
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item $\Delta t_{\mathrm{decode}} \approx -1.05\,\mathrm{s}$ (credible speedup)
\item $\Delta t_{\mathrm{e2e}} \approx -1.02\,\mathrm{s}$
\end{itemize}
Because $t_{\mathrm{upstream}}$ is shared and $t_{\mathrm{compress}}^{\mathrm{A}}=0$,
\[
\Delta t_{\mathrm{e2e}}
  = t_{\mathrm{compress}}^{\mathrm{C}}
  + \Delta t_{\mathrm{decode}}
  \;\Rightarrow\;
  t_{\mathrm{compress}}^{\mathrm{C}}
  \approx (-1.02)-(-1.05)
  \approx \mathbf{20\text{--}25\,\mathrm{ms}}.
\]
So yes: \textbf{there is a compression cost}, but it is $\sim\!0.7\%$ of
$t_{\mathrm{upstream}}$ ($\approx 3.0\,\mathrm{s}$) and $\sim\!1\%$ of baseline
$t_{\mathrm{e2e}}$. Net, compressed arms are \textbf{faster} end-to-end, not
slower.

Why decode speeds up even when tokens rise ($+37$ mean tokens, C vs A):
attention cost tracks \textbf{prefix length} $S$ as well as generated length
$T$. Cutting $S{:}\,640\to 16$--$24$ dominates a modest verbosity tax. ASC on
the Judger removes that tax ($\Delta$ tokens $D{-}C\approx -58$, similar to
$B{-}A$).

\subsection{Memory accounting (important distinction)}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
\toprule
Quantity & Full & Compressed @16 & Notes \\
\midrule
Stored relay after compress
  & $100\,\mathrm{MB}$ & $2.5\,\mathrm{MB}$ & headline $40\times$ \\
Peak GPU during run
  & $36.8\,\mathrm{GB}$ & $34.1\,\mathrm{GB}$ & $\sim\!2.6\,\mathrm{GB}$ drop \\
Upstream compute
  & paid in full & paid in full
  & compressor does \textbf{not} skip latent steps \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Peak still includes model weights + activations while \emph{building} the full
cache; we compress \textbf{after} materialization. The $40\times$ figure is
\textbf{relay residency / Judger prefix memory}, not ``whole process $40\times$
cheaper.'' A separate synthetic-scaffold line removes upstream compute; that is
a different lever (saves FLOPs, historically not KV bytes when kept
full-length).

\subsection{When overhead would matter}

$t_{\mathrm{compress}}$ only becomes comparable to decode if $S$ is huge and
$T$ is tiny, or if you re-compress every decode step (we do not---once per
item). Our scorer is $O(S)$ per layer; an attention-mass H-OBF port would cost
more (needs attentions) and should be re-benchmarked.

\textit{Note:} the harness records \texttt{compress\_s} per row; aggregated
\texttt{report.json} currently omits \texttt{mean\_compress\_s}. The
$20$--$25\,\mathrm{ms}$ figure above is inferred from
$\Delta t_{\mathrm{e2e}}-\Delta t_{\mathrm{decode}}$ and should match a direct
mean of \texttt{compress\_s}.

\section{Experimental design ($2\times 2$ + diagnostics)}

Paired items; same upstream cache reused across Judger arms:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
Arm & Relay & Judger ASC \\
\midrule
A & full & off \\
B & full & on \\
C & obf & off \\
D & obf & on \\
E / F & evict $\pm$ ASC & optional \\
S & shuffled full & off \\
N & none & off \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Primary contrasts:
\begin{align*}
\text{compression tax} &= C - A,\\
\text{ASC}\mid\mathrm{full} &= B - A,\\
\text{ASC}\mid\mathrm{obf} &= D - C,\\
\text{interaction} &= (D-C)-(B-A).
\end{align*}
Target systems story: $\mathrm{acc}(E)\approx\mathrm{acc}(A)$,
$\mathrm{MB}(E)\ll\mathrm{MB}(A)$, and if $T(C)>T(A)$ then ASC restores length
(interaction $\approx 0$ when both cells show the same ASC effect---observed on
GSM8K tokens: interaction $\approx -1.9$ tok, CI covers $0$).

\subsection{Operating rule}
\[
B^\star(\mathrm{task})
  \quad\text{increases with task difficulty / domain shift.}
\]
GSM8K admits $B{=}16$; MATH wants $B\sim 128$ for iso-acc; MedQA should not use
$B\in\{16,32\}$ under this scorer.

\section{What is / is not claimed}

\paragraph{Claimed.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item Relay KV is within-task redundant; position selection yields large MB
cuts at iso-acc on GSM8K (replicated).
\item Task-dependent compression frontier on MATH/MedQA.
\item Compressor wall-clock overhead is $O(\mathrm{numel}(C))$ and empirically
$\ll t_{\mathrm{upstream}},\;|\Delta t_{\mathrm{decode}}|$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Not claimed.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item Elimination of upstream FLOPs (cache is still built once).
\item Universal $40\times$ on hard exams.
\item Byte-faithful per-head attention H-OBF.
\item AIME numbers from the long-decode cache-swap harness (truncation
artifacts).
\end{enumerate}
